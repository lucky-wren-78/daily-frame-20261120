## Correction to the morning edition (v1)

- **The storm was Akvan, not Dana.** The morning edition called the
  cyclone approaching India's east coast "tropical storm Dana" and put
  evacuations at "80,000+". Both were wrong: the system that made
  landfall near Gopalpur on the morning of 20 November 2026 was
  **Cyclone Akvan** (IMD Advisory No. 29; Wikipedia; The Watchers),
  and **more than 300,000 people** had been moved to shelters
  (3.72 lakh, per Odisha's chief secretary). "Dana" was the name of
  an October 2024 cyclone that the morning edition's digest sources
  appear to have recycled. Story 6 in this edition carries the
  corrected name and figure.
