1 · KYIV, UKRAINE — OVERNIGHT LEAD STORY · BALLISTIC MISSILES

Missiles rain on Kyiv in the night

Russia fired a wave of ballistic missiles at Kyiv overnight into Friday, with explosions and fires reported across several districts of the capital. Air-raid alerts sounded across the city and other regions as the missiles came in.

The strike followed a major drone attack on the city the day before, which killed one person and damaged historic buildings and foreign embassies.

It came as Ukraine’s European allies, meeting in Berlin, said they were ready for talks with Russia — with a united position for negotiations.

STRAIT OF HORMUZ · MIDDLE EAST

Tanker burns after hitting a mine off Hormuz

A tanker caught fire after striking a naval mine east of the Strait of Hormuz, Iran’s navy said; the claim could not be independently verified. Brent crude held near $92 as the disruption offset surplus worries.

3
RIYADH · SAUDI ARABIA

Houthis strike four Saudi targets

Yemen’s Houthis said they hit four targets in Saudi Arabia, including Riyadh. The Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted five ballistic missiles and one cruise missile. No casualties were reported.

4
TAIPEI · TAIWAN

Taiwan clamps down on short sellers

New short-selling curbs take effect today after the Taiex fell 4.4% on Thursday. Stocks that drop 3.5% or more cannot be shorted below the close the next day; the daily cap on borrowed-share sales falls to 1%.

5
WASHINGTON · UNITED STATES

Trump pardons baseball’s hit king Pete Rose

President Trump posthumously pardoned Pete Rose, baseball’s all-time hits leader, banned from the game since 1989 over betting on games. The pardon is separate from any Hall of Fame decision.

6
INDIA · SOUTH ASIA

80,000 moved as storm Dana nears

India evacuated more than 80,000 people as tropical storm Dana approached the coast, with shelters opened and fishing boats called back to harbour ahead of landfall.

7
BERLIN · GERMANY

Europe says it is ready to talk to Russia

Ukraine’s European allies, meeting in Berlin, said they have a united position for negotiations with Russia on ending the war — as the overnight strike on Kyiv underlined the stakes.
