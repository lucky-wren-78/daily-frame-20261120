1 · KYIV, UKRAINE — THE OVERNIGHT ASSAULT LEAD STORY · WAR IN UKRAINE

A night of fire over Kyiv: nine dead as 110 missiles and 400 drones hit Ukraine

Russia launched one of the largest air attacks of the war overnight into Friday, firing more than 110 missiles — 27 of them ballistic — and over 400 drones at cities across Ukraine, President Volodymyr Zelensky said. At least nine people were killed and more than two dozen wounded; three bridges across the Dnipro in Kyiv were damaged and power, heating and water were cut in parts of the capital.

The assault came hours after President Vladimir Putin, meeting Donald Trump and Xi Jinping in Shenzhen, promised a painful reply to Ukraine’s destruction of part of the Kerch bridge. Two Russian missiles and a drone fell in eastern Poland during the attack, and Polish and NATO jets were scrambled.

2
STRAIT OF HORMUZ · ENERGY

Tanker ablaze off Hormuz as oil ends the week lower

A tanker caught fire after striking a naval mine east of the Strait of Hormuz, Iran’s navy said — a claim not independently verified. Brent settled at $92.57, up on the day but down for a third straight week as Gulf exports stayed strong.

3
RIYADH · SAUDI ARABIA

Houthi missiles reach Riyadh; tower damaged

Yemen’s Houthis said they struck four targets in Saudi Arabia, including the capital. The Saudi-led coalition said it shot down five ballistic missiles and one cruise missile; the PIF tower in Riyadh was damaged. No casualties were reported.

4
TAIPEI · TAIWAN

Taipei’s worst week since 1991

The Taiex plunged 6.2% to 36,033 on Friday, its third-biggest one-day points fall, capping a 17.5% weekly collapse — the worst since 1991 — as AI doubts spread. New short-selling curbs took effect and the stabilisation fund stepped in.

5
WASHINGTON · UNITED STATES

Trump pardons Pete Rose, 37 years after the ban

President Trump posthumously pardoned Pete Rose, baseball’s all-time hits leader, banned since 1989 for betting on games. The pardon does not by itself put Rose in the Hall of Fame.

6
ODISHA · INDIA

Cyclone Akvan comes ashore; 300,000 moved to safety

Cyclone Akvan crossed the Odisha coast near Gopalpur on Friday morning with winds of 70–80 km/h, gusting to 90. More than 300,000 people had been moved to shelters; 423 rescue teams were deployed and 69 trains cancelled. No deaths were reported in the first assessment.

7
BERLIN · GERMANY

Europe says it is ready to negotiate

Ukraine’s European allies, meeting in Berlin, said they held a united position for talks with Russia on ending the war — even as the night’s attack on Kyiv showed the cost of waiting.
